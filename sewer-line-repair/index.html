Emergency first steps

What to do when a broken sewer line backs up

A collapsed or separated sewer pipe can stop the whole house from draining. Wastewater can also leak into soil under a slab, which is a health and structural concern.

Stop using water, keep people away from the affected area, and call the 24/7 line so the line can be cleared and inspected.

  • Stop all water use and flushing.
  • Keep wastewater away from living areas and pets.
  • Take photos for your insurance claim.
  • Call the 24/7 line for a cable, camera and repair plan.

Once the line is clear, we show you the footage so the repair decision is based on what is actually in the pipe.

Diagnosis

How we confirm damage before recommending repair

Repair starts with a camera and a clean pipe, because debris hides the true condition. We clear the line, run the camera to the cleanout and the street connection, and mark the damage location with a locator so any excavation is exact.

The footage tells us if the pipe is cracked, offset at a joint, belled out, root-invaded or collapsed. A crack and a collapse have very different repair paths and very different costs, so no one should quote before seeing the pipe. Repairs follow the Florida Building Code, Plumbing, the statewide standard built on the International Plumbing Code with Florida changes.

Options

Repair options and when each fits

The right repair depends on damage type, pipe material and access.

OptionBest forTrade-offs.
Spot repairOne short damaged sectionNeeds access to the pipe, so some digging.
Pipe liningA pipe with cracks or root entry but still roundReduces diameter slightly and cannot fix a collapse.
Section replacementA longer damaged runMore excavation, a permanent new pipe.
Full replacementFailing pipe along its lengthHighest cost, longest lifespan.

Lining sometimes avoids digging up landscaping, and replacement is a lasting fix when the pipe is failing along its length.

Pipe material

Cast iron, clay and plastic drains

Many homes planned in the early years of Palm Beach Gardens, which began with John D. MacArthur's 1959 announcement, were built when cast iron drain pipe was common. Cast iron corrodes from the inside over decades, and that scale narrows the pipe and cracks at the bottom.

Newer neighborhoods use plastic pipe, which can still be damaged by settlement or a poor joint. Our camera tells us which material and which condition you have, so you do not have to guess from the year of the house.

If cleaning is the only thing needed, sewer line cleaning is the right page to read. When the camera shows damage, repair is the next step.

Older homes

Cast iron pipe repair in older homes

A cast iron stack or drain that is rusting through can be repaired by replacing the failed section with plastic pipe and the right couplings. We check the whole accessible run, because a failure in one place usually means weakness elsewhere.

Where the pipe runs under a slab, lining or a reroute may be better than breaking concrete. We explain the trade-off and what each choice means for the floor and for cost.
