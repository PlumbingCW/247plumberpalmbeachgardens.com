Emergency first steps

Water heater leaking or sparking: act first

A heater that leaks at the tank body is rarely repairable, and the water can reach drywall or flooring within minutes. Leaks at a fitting, a drain valve or the relief valve are often fixable.

If you smell gas, do not touch switches. Leave the house, then call the gas utility or 911 from outside.

  • Turn the cold water supply valve above the heater clockwise to stop the refill.
  • Switch off the breaker for an electric unit, or set a gas unit to pilot or off.
  • If you smell gas, leave and call the gas utility or 911 first. Florida Public Utilities lists 561-832-0872 and TECO Peoples Gas lists 877-832-6747 on the city emergency contacts page.
  • Call the 24/7 line and tell us whether the water is from the tank, a pipe or the relief valve.

Take a photo of the data plate on the tank. The model and serial numbers let us arrive with the right parts.

Diagnosis

What the symptom usually points to

Most no-hot-water calls come down to a short list of causes.

  • No hot water on an electric tankA tripped breaker, a failed upper or lower heating element or a bad thermostat is the usual cause.
  • No hot water on a gas tankThe pilot may be out, the thermocouple may have failed or the gas control valve may be faulty.
  • Water too hot or not hot enoughA thermostat setting, a failed thermostat or a sediment layer on the bottom of the tank.
  • Rumbling or poppingSediment sits on the bottom and boils under the burner or element.
  • Rusty hot water onlyThe tank interior or the anode rod is breaking down, which often means replacement is near.
Decision

Repair or replace: how we decide

A tank that is under ten years old and leaks at a fitting is worth fixing. A tank that has rusted through, or that needs several parts at once, is usually not. We tell you which category yours is in before any work begins.

Age is only part of it. A water heater in a garage with good ventilation can outlast one in a closet that floods once. We look at the tank, the venting, the temperature and pressure relief valve and the drain pan before giving a recommendation.

If replacement makes sense, our page on water heater replacement explains how that visit works and how same-day scheduling is handled. Manufacturer guidelines call for testing the temperature and pressure relief valve at least once a year, and we check it on every repair visit.

Newer units

Hybrid and heat pump water heaters

Some newer homes use hybrid heat pump water heaters, which pull heat from the surrounding air. They have a compressor, a fan and a condensate line in addition to the usual tank parts. A clogged condensate drain or a dirty air filter can shut one down.

We service these units but they need parts from the manufacturer, so a model number is essential when you call. If the heater is in a garage, the air around it matters, so we check clearances too.

In Palm Beach Gardens

Water heaters in Palm Beach Gardens homes

South Florida homes keep their water heaters in garages, closets and utility rooms, and a leak in an interior closet can reach carpet fast. In slab homes, drain pans and a pan drain line carry small leaks out of the house. We check that line on every visit because a blocked pan line turns a drip into damage.

The Seacoast Utility Authority reports that its drinking water uses chloramine, with 2025 samples averaging 3.7 ppm. Chloramine is a normal disinfectant, but it can wear rubber parts such as seals and flappers faster than plain chlorine does, so we look at rubber fittings during service.
