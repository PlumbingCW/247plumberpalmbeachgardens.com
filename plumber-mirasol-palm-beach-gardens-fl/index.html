From hunting grounds to a golf community

The Country Club at Mirasol was built on land that had been the hunting grounds of the Bonnette Hunt Club, whose lodge dates to 1968. The city's planning map lists Mirasol among its named communities.

The community spreads over roughly 2,300 acres by one listing source. That is a large area, so a street name helps us more than the word Mirasol.

Why a larger home changes the call

In general, a larger home has more bathrooms, more fixtures and longer pipe runs. That can make a small leak harder to find and a water heater question more involved.

If you see a stain, hear water running with every tap closed or notice a high water bill, leak detection finds the source before walls are opened. For hot water that runs out, see water heater installation and replacement.
