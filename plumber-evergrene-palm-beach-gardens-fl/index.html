A newer community with its own timeline

Palm Beach Gardens began with an announcement in 1959, but Evergrene belongs to a later chapter. Listing data suggests most homes were built in the 2000s.

That timeline is useful in one practical way. Tank water heaters commonly last about 8 to 12 years. If the heater in your home is the one the builder installed, it may be well past that range, so ask us to check its age.

What ordinary calls look like

A heater that stops making hot water, a slow drain, a toilet that keeps running or a faucet that drips are the everyday jobs. See water heater replacement, drain cleaning and faucet repair.
