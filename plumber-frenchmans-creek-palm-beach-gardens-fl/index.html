Two kinds of address

A public neighborhood listing describes the gated Frenchman's Creek Beach and Country Club as about 600 homes across 700 acres. Homes inside range from carriage homes to large waterfront estates, often in a Spanish Revival style.

Outside the gates are smaller subdivisions with ranch-style homes. The city's planning map lists Frenchmans Creek and, separately, Frenchmans Reserve, so confirm which one you mean.

What age does and does not tell us

A median build year near 1988 means many homes here are several decades old. Age alone does not tell us what is inside the walls, because pipes are repaired and replaced over time.

A pressure test, a camera view or a look at a visible fitting tells us more. See leak detection for hidden leaks and water line repair for the line that brings water to the house.

Coastal homes and exposed fittings

In general, homes near salt water and humid air can see corrosion on exposed metal fittings, valves and supply connectors. That is general plumbing information and not a claim about any one house.

If you see green or white crust on a valve or a slow drip at a connector, have it checked. If a section of pipe needs to be replaced, repiping may be one option to discuss.

What we could not confirm

We could not confirm which sections have been repiped, the pipe materials used or the exact boundary between the gated club and the neighborhoods outside it.

Assumptions about old pipes are cheap. A recorded camera run or a test is how we find the facts.
