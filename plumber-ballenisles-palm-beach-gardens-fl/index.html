A site with a long local history

The City of Palm Beach Gardens notes that in 1972 the PGA moved from the property that became the J.D.M. Country Club, now BallenIsles. The golf courses here once hosted the PGA headquarters, and the community took the BallenIsles name in 1989.

That history explains the layout. Homes were added around the golf courses in groups, which is why you hear subdivision names more often than the community name.

The common calls are ordinary ones. A water heater stops making hot water, a drain slows down, a toilet runs or a stain appears below a bathroom. See water heater repair, drain cleaning and toilet repair.
