Basics

How a tankless unit differs from a tank

A tankless heater has no storage. When a tap opens, a flow sensor fires a burner or element and the water leaves hot. That removes standby heat loss and the risk of a tank flood, but it puts all the demand on one device at once.

If two showers and a washer run together, a unit that is too small will fall behind. We size by flow rate and temperature rise, not by tank gallons, and we check that your home has the gas supply or electrical capacity the unit needs. The maker's installation guidelines set the minimum gas supply and the approved vent materials, and the flush interval in the manual is the one we follow.

Problems

Tankless problems we fix

  • Error code on the displayThe code points to ignition, flow, venting or sensor trouble. We read it before touching parts.
  • Lukewarm water or temperature swingsScale on the heat exchanger or a clogged inlet filter is common.
  • No hot water at low flowSome units need a minimum flow to fire, and a dirty filter reduces it.
  • Noisy operationScale, a failing fan or a venting restriction can cause it.
  • Cold sandwich effectWater already in the pipes between uses runs cold before hot arrives.

Maintenance

Flushing and descaling

Mineral scale builds on the heat exchanger over time and cuts flow and heat. Flushing runs a descaling solution through the unit in a loop and then rinses it. We also clean the inlet screen.

The Seacoast Utility Authority treats its water with nanofiltration and low-pressure reverse osmosis at its Hood Road plant. Its 2025 water quality report does not list a hardness figure, so we do not guess at scale rates. We look at your unit and recommend an interval from what we see.

Safety

Gas safety and venting

Gas tankless units need a sized gas line and a proper vent. A sticker on the unit lists the minimum gas supply it needs. If you smell gas around the unit, leave the house and call the gas utility or 911 from outside.

Condensing models drain acidic condensate, which needs a neutralizer or a safe drain. We check that drain during every service because a clogged condensate line shuts the unit down.
