Who we are

Palm Beach Gardens 24/7 Plumbing answers a phone line around the clock for plumbing problems in Palm Beach Gardens and nearby towns, and we dispatch plumbers to homes and businesses in the city and nearby towns.

We keep our promises narrow on purpose. We do not guarantee an arrival time, because traffic, storms and call volume change it. We do promise a clear price before work starts.

How the 24/7 line works

When you call, a person asks what is happening and what you can see. We tell you what to shut off, and we dispatch a plumber the same day when a slot is open.

The price is explained before any work begins. If the job turns out to be larger than expected, we stop and explain the new scope.

Why we focus on Palm Beach Gardens

The city has a specific history and a specific set of homes. John D. MacArthur announced it on March 20, 1959, with a plan for about 4,000 acres and 55,000 residents on former dairy land. The PGA moved in during the mid 1960s, and PGA National began construction in 1978.

Those dates matter to a plumber. Houses built in different decades use different pipes and fixtures, and the right repair for a 1960s drain line is not the right repair for a recent one. Local knowledge shortens a visit because we know what to expect when you say which part of the city you live in.

How we write our guides

Our service pages and blog guides are written for this city. We use primary sources such as the Seacoast Utility Authority water quality report and the City of Palm Beach Gardens website for local facts. Where a fact is not published, such as water hardness, we say so and suggest testing your tap.

When a permit question comes up, we do not guess. The city's Building Division at 561-799-4210 decides what applies to your address, and we say so on every page where it matters.

What to expect when a plumber arrives

The plumber confirms the problem with you, looks at it and explains what the fix involves before touching anything. You hear the price and the scope. If you say yes, work starts. If you say no, we explain what you owe, if anything, before you decide, and we put it in plain words.

We wear shoe covers inside, protect floors where we work and clean up before we leave. If a part is not on the truck, we say so and tell you when we can return. We would rather give you a straight answer than a hopeful one.

A note on honesty

Plumbing quotes are easiest to mistrust when they are vague. We aim to be specific: what is wrong, what we will do, what it costs and what could change the number.

If a camera or a pressure test shows something we did not expect, we pause and explain. You are never asked to approve a surprise after the work is done.

How we handle follow-up

If a repair has a problem after we leave, call the same number. We would rather come back and fix it than have you wonder whether to call. We keep notes on each job so the next plumber knows what was done and why.

For larger projects such as a repipe or a sewer repair, we explain what to watch for in the first weeks and who to call if something changes. Good plumbing work should be boring after the day it is finished, and we aim for that.
