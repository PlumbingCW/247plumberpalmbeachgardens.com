When something is wrong right now

What to do while you wait for an emergency plumber

The first minutes of a plumbing emergency decide how much damage follows. Most of the steps cost nothing and take under a minute.

Do the one that matches what you see, then call the 24/7 line so we can walk you through the rest.

  • Water spraying or pooling: close the main shutoff, usually near the front hose bib or at the meter box.
  • Toilet or tub overflowing: stop flushing and running water, and close the valve behind the toilet.
  • No hot water with a wet floor: shut the cold valve above the water heater and switch off its power.
  • Gas smell: leave the house and call the gas utility or 911 from outside. Do not touch switches.

None of this replaces a plumber. It keeps the problem small until one arrives. Our burst pipe repair and drain cleaning pages go into more detail.

Palm Beach Gardens plumbing

Plumbing in a city that began as a 1959 plan

Palm Beach Gardens was announced by John D. MacArthur on March 20, 1959, and the 2020 census counted 59,182 residents.

City announced.1959
2020 population.59,182
Average elevation.16 ft
Yearly rainfall.66 in

The city grew from a plan for about 4,000 acres and 55,000 residents on former dairy land, with the PGA moving in around 1964 and PGA National beginning construction in 1978. Houses built in those different decades carry different plumbing, and that is what we look at first on a call.

Seacoast Utility Authority supplies water for much of the city. Its 2025 water quality report describes wells, nanofiltration and low-pressure reverse osmosis at the Hood Road plant, with chloramine averaging 3.7 ppm. The report lists no hardness figure, so if scale worries you, test your tap or ask the utility.

Rain matters too. The city sees about 66 inches a year on roughly 113 days, and heavy storms can overload yard drains and clog cleanouts with sand and leaves. We clear and camera those lines as part of a drain call.
