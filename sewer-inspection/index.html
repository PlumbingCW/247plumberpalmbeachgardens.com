Purpose

What an inspection shows

A camera inspection reveals the pipe material, the joints, cracks, offsets, roots, grease and any section holding water. The picture comes with a locator reading so we can mark a defect on the surface if repair is needed.

You get the footage and a plain explanation. That lets you compare quotes, decide on repair, or learn that the line is in fine shape and the problem is somewhere else. Utilities and engineers often grade pipe defects with the NASSCO PACP coding standard, so ask us for footage you can share with them.

When to book

When an inspection is worth scheduling

  • Before buying a homeA sale inspection cannot see underground drains. A camera can, and it shows if a costly repair is waiting.
  • After repeat clogsIf a line keeps backing up, the camera shows why.
  • Before a remodelChanging a bathroom or adding a fixture adds load to a line you have never seen.
  • After a backupConfirm the pipe is sound after a cleaning.
  • Slow drains in several fixturesA camera finds a shared cause.
Process

How the inspection works

Locate the access

We use a cleanout, a pulled toilet or a roof vent.

Run the camera

The head moves down the pipe and records video.

Mark findings

A locator shows depth and position for any defect.

Review together

We explain the footage and what, if anything, needs action.

In Palm Beach Gardens

Buying an older home in Palm Beach Gardens

The city began with John D. MacArthur's 1959 announcement and has homes from several decades, including the PGA National development that began construction in 1978. A house from any of those eras can have a drain line that a general inspector never saw.

A pre-purchase camera run takes less time than most repairs and gives you a report to use in negotiation. If the footage shows damage, the cost and options appear before closing, not after.

Older lines may need repair or lining, and a line that is just dirty may only need cleaning.

What we will not claim

We cannot see through the ground, and a camera only shows the line it travels. If a section is inaccessible or blocked, we say so rather than imply the whole system is fine.

A clean video of one run does not mean every branch in the house is clean.
