Emergency first steps

What to do when a disposal is leaking or jammed

A leak from the bottom of the unit usually means the housing has failed and the disposal must be replaced. A leak at the top or side may be a gasket, a drain hose or the sink flange.

Never put a hand into a disposal, even when it is off.

  • Turn off the disposal switch and the breaker.
  • Do not use hands or tools inside the chamber. Use the hex key in the bottom to free a jam only if the power is off.
  • Close the supply valves if water is leaking under the sink.
  • Call the 24/7 line and tell us if the sink is also backing up.

A disposal that backs up the sink may be clogged downstream of the unit. See drain cleaning.

Symptoms

What the symptom means

  • Hum but no spinThe blades are jammed. Power off, then free with a hex key.
  • Nothing at allThe reset button is tripped or the unit has no power.
  • Leak from the bottomThe housing is cracked. Replace the disposal.
  • Leak at the sink flangeThe flange seal has failed.
  • Bad smellFood residue in the chamber or drain line.

Care

What to keep out of a disposal

Grease, coffee grounds in large amounts, fibrous vegetables, pasta and rice swell or stick and cause clogs. Bones and fruit pits can jam the blades. Run cold water while it works and for a bit afterward.

A disposal is not a trash can. The kitchen drain downstream is only an inch and a half wide, and repeated clogs there are often traced back to what goes through the unit. According to most disposal makers' guidelines, cold water should run during grinding and for a few seconds afterward.

Installation

Installing a new disposal

A new disposal needs the sink flange, a mounting ring, a drain connection and an electrical connection through a cord or a hardwired box. The dishwasher drain hose connects at a knockout plug that must be removed or the dishwasher will not drain.

We size the unit to your use. A basic half-horsepower model suits light use. Larger households may prefer more power and sound insulation.

In Palm Beach Gardens

Disposals and local drains

In Palm Beach Gardens homes with older kitchen drains, grease often builds up in the branch beyond the disposal. A new disposal does not fix a drain that is narrow with grease. If the sink backs up even with a working disposal, the line needs cleaning.

Seacoast Utility Authority asks customers to keep grease and wipes out of drains. That helps your own kitchen branch as well as the utility system.

Choosing

Choosing a disposal that fits

Continuous feed models run while a switch is held on. Batch feed models run with a stopper in place and are often considered safer around small children. Power ranges from a quarter horsepower to more than one horsepower.

Sound insulation, grind stages and corrosion-resistant parts affect price and noise. We will help you choose a size that fits your cabinet and your cooking habits.
