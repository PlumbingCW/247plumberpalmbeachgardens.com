Palm Beach Gardens

Palm Beach Gardens covers about 59 square miles of Palm Beach County and had 59,182 residents in the 2020 census. We serve it from its center, which keeps travel short for neighborhoods near PGA Boulevard, Military Trail and Northlake Boulevard.

Wikipedia lists ZIP codes 33403, 33408, 33410, 33412, 33418 and 33420 for the city area. We confirm each address by phone because ZIP boundaries are not the same as city limits. See our plumbing services for what we do.

North Palm Beach

North Palm Beach is the village next to us along US 1. It has its own page at emergency plumber in North Palm Beach because of its age, its waterfront and its own village permit office.

The village was incorporated in 1956 and had 13,162 residents in the 2020 census. Its main ZIP code is 33408, and homes there are on average older than in much of Palm Beach Gardens, which changes what we expect to find in drain and supply lines.

Neighborhoods inside Palm Beach Gardens

Six named communities have their own pages: PGA National, BallenIsles, Mirasol, Evergrene, Frenchman's Creek and Garden Oaks. Each one appears on the City of Palm Beach Gardens planning map.

Other communities exist inside the city, and we have not written about them because we could not confirm enough about each. If yours is not listed, call and give the street.

How plumbing differs across the city

Palm Beach Gardens is a city of planned communities, and each one tends to have its own era and pipe materials. The area around PGA National, which began construction in 1978, differs from newer gated communities farther west. Resort and club communities such as BallenIsles and Mirasol have their own associations and rules for outside work.

Commercial areas such as The Gardens Mall, Legacy Place and Midtown along PGA Boulevard have heavy water and drain use from restaurants and retail. Palm Beach State College's Eissey Campus and other institutional sites have their own facilities teams, but we can help with after-hours emergencies where a contractor is allowed.

The practical point is simple. Tell us the community or the street when you call, and we will match the plumber and the parts to the age of the house.

Why we do not list every town

A long list of towns on a page can look helpful, but it can also hide the fact that no one on the other end can reach them. We would rather tell you what we do than copy a list of places we have never driven to. If you do not see your town, the phone call is the fastest answer.

When we cannot reach you, we will say so and, where we can, suggest what to ask the next plumber you call, such as whether they are licensed and whether they quote a price before starting.

Nearby towns we also reach

Juno Beach, Lake Park, Jupiter and other towns around us sometimes call. We do not have a page for each, and we do not promise arrival times. If you are in one of them, call and we will say honestly whether we can reach you.

Seacoast Utility Authority, which serves Palm Beach Gardens, North Palm Beach, Juno Beach, Lake Park and parts of the county, also posts neighborhood project notices. Those notices can explain a sudden pressure change.

Who to call for what

Not every problem belongs to a plumber. For a gas smell, leave the house and call the gas utility or 911 from outside. The city lists Florida Public Utilities at 561-832-0872 and TECO Peoples Gas at 877-832-6747. For a power problem, FPL lists 800-468-8243.

For water or sewer service on the utility side of the meter, Seacoast Utility Authority publishes an emergency line at 561-627-2900. For a problem inside your property line, call us. We will tell you if the cause turns out to be on the utility side, and what to tell them.

What happens after you call about an address

We ask for the street address, the cross street or community and the nature of the problem. If the address is inside the city, we arrange service for it. If it is in a nearby town, we tell you plainly whether we can reach you that day or whether another plumber would serve you faster.

We would rather lose a call than waste your time with a promise we cannot keep. That is why this page does not list a long run of towns. It lists the places we actually serve and tells you how to ask about the rest.
