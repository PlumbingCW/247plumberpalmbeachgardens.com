How the community grew

Ground was broken in 1978 on land bought from John D. MacArthur, the same developer who announced Palm Beach Gardens in 1959. The plan called for about 6,900 homes over roughly 15 years.

A community built in many sections over many years is why the community name alone tells a plumber little. Your street, your building or your neighborhood name is more useful on the phone.

Attached homes and who owns which pipe

Many PGA National homes share walls with a neighbor. A leak in one unit can reach the next, and an association may own some of the supply or drain lines that serve a building.

Your association documents decide which lines are yours. Ask before you pay for work on a line that may not be yours, and tell the dispatcher what you find so we can plan the visit.
