Emergency first steps

A failed tank cannot wait

A tank that leaks from its body will keep leaking, and the water goes wherever the floor slopes. A garage floor drains it outside. A closet in a slab house sends it into walls and carpet.

Stop the refill first. Then stop the energy source.

  • Close the cold water shutoff valve on the pipe above the heater.
  • Turn off the breaker for an electric unit or set the gas valve to off.
  • Open a hot tap in the house to relieve pressure and let the tank drain slowly if you are able to attach a hose.
  • Call the 24/7 line and give us the tank size and fuel type.

If a gas smell is present, leave the house and call the gas utility or 911 from outside. Do not try to relight anything.

Sizing

Choosing the right replacement

Tank size should match your household, not the size of the old tank. A family of five and a couple use very different amounts of hot water. We ask about showers, tubs and laundry habits and size from that.

Fuel type is the next decision. Swapping like for like is the simplest and fastest. Switching from gas to electric or to a hybrid heat pump changes venting, wiring and sometimes the permit steps, so we talk through the tradeoffs before ordering. Both the IPC and the Florida code built on it expect a relief valve with a safe discharge line, so we run one on every replacement.

Permits

Permits, codes and local rules

Water heater replacement may need a permit depending on the work, and permit rules vary, so the safest step is to ask the Palm Beach Gardens Building Division at 561-799-4210. Its office hours are Monday to Friday, 8 a.m. to 4:30 p.m., and permits are handled through an online system.

Florida adopted the 8th Edition (2023) of the Florida Building Code, Plumbing, and we install to that code. We do not guess at your property's needs. If a permit applies, we explain the steps before work starts.

In Palm Beach Gardens

Where heaters fail in local homes

Many Palm Beach Gardens homes were built from the 1960s onward after John D. MacArthur's plan opened the area, and heaters in older utility closets sit in tight spaces where moisture builds up. Garage heaters face heat and humidity, which speeds corrosion on fittings and the tank bottom.

If a heater is more than a decade old, replacing it before it fails is cheaper than replacing it after water reaches the floor. We can inspect the anode rod and the drain valve on a service visit and give you an honest read on remaining life.
