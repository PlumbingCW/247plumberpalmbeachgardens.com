Emergency first steps

Sewer backup: what to do first

A main sewer backup brings wastewater through the lowest drain in the house. In a slab home that is usually a shower, a tub or a floor drain.

Every minute of water use makes it worse, so the first move is to stop all water.

  • Stop using every fixture, including the washer and dishwasher.
  • Do not flush. The toilet will fill the floor if the line is blocked.
  • Photograph any water on the floor for your records.
  • Call the 24/7 line and ask for a main-line cleaning with a camera.

If a lot of wastewater has reached carpet or drywall, tell the dispatcher so a restoration contractor can be arranged after the line is clear.

The system

What the main line is and where it fails

Your sewer line runs from the house cleanout or the lowest fixture out to the connection at the street or the utility system. Branch lines from each bathroom and kitchen join it, so one blockage anywhere downstream affects them all.

The usual causes are grease, flushed wipes, roots entering joints, and sags where soil has shifted. A camera tells us which one before we choose a tool, so the clean-out is a fix and not a guess. Model codes such as the IPC call for cleanouts at set points on a building drain, and a missing one is worth adding while the line is open.

Symptoms

Signs of a main line problem rather than a branch clog

  • Toilets and tubs on the lowest level back upWater takes the easiest path out, and that is the lowest opening.
  • Gurgling from drains when the washer runsThe washer pushes a slug of water that the main cannot take.
  • Water in the shower when the toilet flushesFixtures share the same main, so they react together.
  • Bubbling in a floor drainAir displaced by water is escaping upstream.
  • Odor near a cleanoutA partial blockage can release gas through the cleanout cap.
Process

How we clean a main line

Find the access point

Local utility

Sewer service in Palm Beach Gardens and North Palm Beach

The Seacoast Utility Authority serves customers across Palm Beach Gardens, North Palm Beach, Juno Beach, Lake Park and parts of unincorporated Palm Beach County. Its website warns customers that flushable-labeled wipes clog sewer lines and damage pumps at its plants.

That matters at your house because a wipe that clears your toilet can snag at a joint downstream. When we clean a main line we bring wipes up in a surprising share of the jobs, so the household habit is worth changing even when the pipe is otherwise sound.

Property owners connected to a utility sewer are generally responsible for the line on their own lot. If a blockage might be on the utility side, we will tell you what the camera shows so you can call the utility with facts.

After the clean

Prevention that actually works

Keep wipes, grease and paper towels out of drains, and dispose of cooking fat in the trash. If roots are a problem, a camera inspection every so often shows how fast they are returning. A yearly look makes sense for older lines with a history of clogs.

If your line has backed up more than once, ask about hydro jetting and a sewer camera inspection so you know the pipe condition before the next backup.
