Emergency first steps

Water everywhere: first five minutes

Burst pipes can flood a home quickly, and water can reach outlets and fixtures. The priority is the water supply, then the electrical safety of the area.

Do not wade through standing water near outlets or appliances.

  • Close the main water valve at the house, usually near the front hose bib or inside the garage.
  • If water is near electrical outlets, switch off the breaker for that area only if you can reach it dry.
  • Open a low faucet to drain remaining pressure from the lines.
  • Call the 24/7 line and tell us where the water is coming from and whether the main closed.

Take photos and short videos before cleanup. They help with any insurance claim.

Causes

Why pipes burst in South Florida

Hard freezes are not common here, but cold fronts occasionally bring lows into the 30s and 40s, and an exposed pipe or a hose bib can fail in a cold snap. Most bursts here come from other causes: corrosion, high pressure, a failed fitting or a pipe damaged during construction work.

Water pressure above about 80 psi strains pipes and fixtures. A pressure regulator and gauge cost little compared with a burst, and we check pressure during repairs. ASTM standards cover PEX, CPVC and copper tube, and we use fittings listed for the pipe that is actually in your wall.

Materials

Pipe materials and repair

Copper, PEX, CPVC and galvanized steel each need different fittings. Mixing metals can cause corrosion, so we use the correct transition fittings. Older galvanized pipe often fails in several places, which is when a repipe makes more sense than patching.

If one burst is the third failure on the same line, we recommend looking at repiping instead of repeated repairs.

Aftermath

After the repair: drying and next steps

Once the water stops, wet drywall and flooring need to dry within a day or two to avoid mold. A restoration contractor can dry the structure, and your insurer can tell you which firms they work with.

We do not do structural drying ourselves, so we tell you plainly when a restoration company should be called. We make the plumbing safe and leave notes for your adjuster.
